# David Gluzman

Product, data and AI for problems too tangled to start.

Twenty years in product and design, on data layers I built myself. I founded the Global Wine Database, one standard for the world's wine data, merged it with Wine Folly in 2019 and ran Wine Folly as CEO for seven years. Before that I co-founded BlackSquare: direct-to-consumer commerce for beverage alcohol, with each market's licensing and tax built into the platform, on four continents.

Now I do fractional CTO and product work as Mindshare and build apps of my own, with AI in the loop from the spec to the live product.

## What I'm building

- [forktales.app](https://forktales.app): a private recipe network for friends. Post a plate, ask for the recipe, fork it. AI reads a recipe card in seconds.
- [abettercamper.com](https://abettercamper.com): every place in British Columbia you can legally spend the night, reconciled from ten public sources and ranked for your dates by weather, smoke, fire bans and the drive.

## Archives I brought back

- [r4nt.com](https://r4nt.com): the Calgary online magazine I started in 2001 and ran for nine and a half years, across eight CMS versions I built myself.
- [tbcc.gluzman.com](https://tbcc.gluzman.com): five years of junior racing in Alberta and Italy, rebuilt from my father's binders and the website I made at sixteen.
